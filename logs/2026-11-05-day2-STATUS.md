## 2. What was settled, and the correction it forced

### The threshold question has a sourced negative answer

`logs/2026-11-05-STATUS.md` §7 named the single question that would have
changed yesterday most: is `f_v0` the volume fraction below critical density or
below some other threshold, and is the threshold published as a number?

**There is no numerical threshold and there cannot be one.** `[SOURCED]`
arXiv:0712.3984 §5 Eq. (7) is a **denial**: `rho_cr != 3 H_av^2 / (8 pi G)` in
an inhomogeneous cosmology, "as the average Hubble parameter is related to the
spatial scale of averaging and the clock chosen". Finite infinity is defined
kinematically instead, as the scale over which the average expansion vanishes,
with `rho_cr` the density there. So the demarcation is a condition on motion,
local and scale-dependent, and the model explicitly rejects the one formula
that would make it a density number.

The relation sign needed two routes: `pdftotext` dropped the glyph and the
`ar5iv` HTML gives `!=`. Settled independently from inside the PDF by the same
section's "with equality in (7)", which only parses if (7) is not an equality.

**A later session should not go looking for that number.** The search cannot
succeed, and knowing that is worth more than the search.

### The premise WP-4 could not verify is now supported three ways

1. `[SOURCED]` arXiv:0909.0749 §II identifies the wall phase observationally
   as "all extended structures that contain galaxy clusters, namely sheets,
   filaments and knots" - which is what VoidFinder's wall galaxies trace. The
   premise is close to the model's own instruction for mapping itself onto a
   galaxy survey. What stays `[INFERENCE]` is narrower: whether VoidFinder's
   third-nearest-neighbour cut implements that mapping well.
2. Quantified failure condition. Under the FLRW-style reading Eq. (7) denies -
   the only reading in which the threshold is a number, hence the only one in
   which the premise can fail numerically - the premise needs
   `delta_m > +0.74` at worst, `> +12.6` at best. A wall-level overdensity
   inside a region selected for having no wall galaxies. `[INFERENCE]`;
   premise: linear bias `b > 0` on 10-30 h^-1 Mpc scales.
3. arXiv:0909.0749 attributes the gap between `f_v0` and catalogued void
   volume to **minivoids**, which arXiv:2202.01226's 10 h^-1 Mpc minimum
   radius excludes by construction. This route needs no bias assumption and no
   threshold at all.

### The correction: a lower bound excludes from below

**`findings/WP-4-void-fraction.md` §6 and `verify/wp4_fv0_comparison.py` apply
the bound in one direction only, and that is wrong.** They test whether each
fitted `f_v0` falls inside the measured interval `[0.448, 0.626]` and conclude
`f_v0` is "unconstrained by measurement". But §4 establishes the measurement as
a **lower bound**, and a lower bound excludes everything below it. The
interval's low end is the edge-*excluded* variant, a weaker bound on the same
quantity, not a second measurement of it.

| bound | excludes |
|---|---|
| 0.6228 (Planck2018, edge included) | `f_v0 = 0.550`, `0.557` |
| 0.4482 (Planck2018, edge excluded) | nothing |

So the exclusion is **real and entirely conditional on the edge treatment**,
already named as the dominant systematic at 17.5 points. Both halves are
asserted in `verify/wp4_critical_threshold.py` so neither can vanish quietly.
`f_v0 = 0.627` clears the bound by `+0.0042`, less than this pipeline's own
`0.0148` disagreement with the published fraction: not excluded, not
distinguished.

### K5 - re-tested, still does not fire

Eight of ten fitted values sit at or above the strongest available bound, so
"excludes **every** fitted `f_v0`" is not met. Yesterday's conclusion is
unchanged; its reason is replaced. Not "the measurement constrains nothing",
but "the measurement excludes the bottom of the fitted range, conditional on
the systematic that dominates it".

### Two routes converge

The two prescriptions the bound excludes are the same two of arXiv:1410.3470
Table 2's three whose dressed `H_0` (56.148, 56.774) falls below timescape's
own admissible floor of 57. Two independent routes reject the same two of
three. Only `A(H_bar_dec)` is simultaneously consistent with the measured void
volume and with timescape's own `H_0` floor, and it survives the bound by less
than this pipeline's error.

## 8. What I would have done differently with one question answered

**The question: is there a second void catalogue, at a smaller minimum radius,
with a published volume fraction computed against its own survey mask?**

Everything WP-4 can now say reduces to one shape: the measured fraction is a
floor, the model needs more volume than the floor supplies, and the model says
the extra is in voids below the catalogue's 10 h^-1 Mpc cut. That is a complete
argument and it is unfalsifiable with this dataset, because the dataset cannot
see the population the argument turns on. One catalogue reaching below 10
h^-1 Mpc, with its volume fraction computed the same way, would convert the
floor into a two-sided measurement and make K5 decidable in either direction.

Yesterday's answer to this section was "a numerical threshold for `f_v0`". That
question is now **answered and closed** - there is no such number, by design -
which is why the useful question has moved. The threshold was the right thing
to ask and the answer redirected the package rather than advancing it.
